---
name: incident-repair
description: Diagnose and repair an observed implementation or delivery failure within the user's existing scope and project recovery authority.
---

Inspect the exact failed revision/run and minimum relevant evidence; distinguish a code defect from missing permission or an external failure. Diagnosis alone does not authorize implementation. Never expose credentials or weaken a security, validation, or cost control to obtain a pass.

Use at most three meaningful attempts per repair generation. After two ineffective attempts, retire the unchanged action and reconsider the mechanism or verified preconditions. Permit one unchanged rerun only with evidence of an external or flaky cause. Preserve action history across continuations; a renamed strategy never resets its budget. Honor any tighter project/time/cost budget.

An unsuccessful action does not disprove its diagnosis. The same supported cause may continue with a different repair mechanism or materially changed, verified preconditions; relabeling the same action or citing unchanged evidence does not qualify.

Before recovery, verify the exact affected target, accepted release, current installation, recoverability, and authority. Use only the configured recovery path; ambiguous or newer state blocks mutation. When blocked or the budget ends, retain the failure fingerprint, attempted actions, evidence, next discriminating action, and resume trigger. Report unresolved runtime state truthfully.
